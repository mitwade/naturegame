How to play

Place hexagonal terrain tiles onto the shared board to complete geometric patterns (Triangle = 1pt, Elbow = 2pt, Straight Line = 3pt) shown on your Nature Cards, then claim them from your hand or the public Bank. Most points after 3 rounds wins.
